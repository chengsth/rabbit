Sunny Day

A little game with no losing, only nice things.

  • Tap the happy things floating by: 💖 ⭐ 🎈 🦋
  • Tap a grey rain cloud 🌧️ to turn it into a rainbow.
  • Tap the grass to plant a flower 🌷
  • Fill the happy meter and a new friend comes to dance.

Sound on makes it nicer. Tap quickly to play a tune.
